DreamSacco

A modern, digital solution transforming traditional Ugandan savings groups (SACCOs) into a transparent, disciplined, and efficient experience for youth.

Group 14 · Makerere University

The Problem

Many young people struggle to maintain consistent saving habits due to messy, manual record-keeping in notebooks or WhatsApp groups. Lack of transparency around group funds, disputes over who has paid, and the complexity of calculating loans and interest all make traditional SACCOs slow, error-prone, and hard to trust.

The Solution

DreamSacco takes everything that works about traditional savings groups — collective power, shared accountability — and fixes what doesn't. Every contribution, loan, and group decision is tracked digitally, transparently, and in real time, so members can trust the numbers without trusting each other's memory.

Key Features

Private & Public Groups

Create or join savings groups, with admin approval flows and credit-score risk flags on every join request.

Transparency Dashboard

Real-time group balance, goal progress, and a clear view of who has paid this month.

Credit Scoring Engine

An intelligent scoring system based on contribution consistency and repayment history determines loan limits and interest rates.

Smart Loan System

Automated loan requests, auto-generated repayment schedules, and admin approval — ranked by credit score.

Goal Tracking

Visual progress bars, milestone celebrations, and a What-If Calculator to project savings growth.

Secure by Design

Firebase Authentication with OTP verification and multi-factor checks on sensitive actions.

Real-Time Notifications

Stay updated on join approvals, loan status, and group activity as it happens.

Share-Based Groups

Groups can be structured around percentage-based shares, with contribution scaled to ownership.

Technologies

Flutter Firebase Authentication Cloud Firestore Firebase Cloud Messaging Dart
