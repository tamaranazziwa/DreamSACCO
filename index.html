DreamSacco

Turning traditional savings groups into a transparent, disciplined, digital experience for Ugandan youth.

View on GitHub

The Problem

Many young people struggle to maintain consistent saving habits due to manual, messy record-keeping in notebooks or WhatsApp groups. Lack of transparency around group funds and the complexity of managing loans and interest calculations make traditional SACCOs slow and hard to trust.

Key Features

Group Management

Create private or public savings groups with admin approval flows and credit-score risk flags.

Transparency Dashboard

Real-time group balance, goal progress, and a clear view of who has paid this month.

Credit Scoring Engine

Contribution consistency and repayment history determine loan limits and interest rates.

Smart Loan System

Automated requests, auto-generated repayment schedules, and admin approval.

Goal Tracking

Visual progress bars, milestone celebrations, and a What-If savings calculator.

Secure by Design

Firebase Authentication with OTP verification on sensitive actions.

How It Works

1

Create or Join a Group

Start a private or public savings group, or join one via invite.

2

Contribute Monthly

Pay straight from the app via MTN MoMo, tracked automatically.

3

Build Your Credit Score

Consistency unlocks higher loan limits and better rates.

4

Request & Repay Loans

Get an auto-generated schedule and track it to completion.

5

Track Progress Together

Watch goals grow and celebrate milestones as a group.
