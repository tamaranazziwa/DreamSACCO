DreamSacco

DreamSacco is a modern, digital solution designed to transform traditional savings groups (SACCOs) into a transparent, disciplined, and efficient experience for Ugandan youth.

The Problem

Many young people struggle to maintain consistent saving habits due to manual, messy record-keeping in notebooks or WhatsApp groups. Lack of transparency around group funds and the complexity of managing loans and interest calculations make traditional SACCOs slow and hard to trust.

Key Features

Group Management: Create private or public savings groups, with admin approval flows and credit-score risk flags on every join request.

Transparency Dashboard: Real-time group balance, goal progress, and a clear view of who has paid this month.

Credit Scoring Engine: An intelligent scoring system based on contribution consistency and repayment history determines loan limits and interest rates.

Smart Loan System: Automated loan requests, auto-generated repayment schedules, and admin approval — ranked by credit score.

Goal Tracking: Visual progress bars, milestone celebrations, and a What-If Calculator to project savings growth.

Security: Firebase Authentication with OTP verification and multi-factor checks on sensitive actions.

How It Works

1. Create or join a savings group, private or public.

2. Contribute monthly straight from the app via MTN MoMo.

3. Build your credit score through consistent contributions and repayments.

4. Request loans with auto-generated repayment schedules.

5. Track group and personal goals together in real time.

Technologies

Flutter, Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Firebase Cloud Messaging.
